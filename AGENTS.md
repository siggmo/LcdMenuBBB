General: Even if you're capable of creating a technically sophisticated and superior solution, remember that maintainability, ease of onboarding and human understanding are main project requirements. The simplest solution that fulfills the requirements, while maintaining API contract and safety, is usually the best! The current repo state is the only thing that matters. Do not record how we got there when changing things. Important design decisions will be added manually to the README.md or in the list below, by the developers themselves.
